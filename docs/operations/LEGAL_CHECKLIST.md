# Closed beta legal checklist

- [x] Publish clearly marked pre-release drafts of terms of use, privacy notice, cookie/session notice and prohibited-goods rules.
- [ ] Replace the drafts with operator-approved documents before inviting external users.
- [x] Provide an authenticated machine-readable self-service copy of account-linked data.
- [x] Provide a durable, auditable account-erasure request and operator decision workflow.
- Document data controller, purposes, retention, deletion/export requests and incident contacts.
- Verify consent text and age restrictions; review moderation appeals and law-enforcement request handling.
- Real payments remain disabled until business registration, provider onboarding, receipts, refunds, taxation and financial retention are approved.
- Confirm rights to demo images, trademarks and user-uploaded content processing.
- Complete a jurisdiction-specific legal review before public launch; this checklist is not legal advice.
